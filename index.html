OUR LOVE STORY

HOW WE MET

We were classmates back in elementary school, but our real story sparked when I greeted her on New Year's Day. After chatting for a while, we met up to go jogging together at IL Corso, that's the very first time we saw each other again after all those years.

THE PROPOSAL

It happened at a serene mountain resort that we had all to ourselves. While playing pickleball, I pretended to get injured. The moment she ran over worried to check on me, I got down on one knee and asked her to marry me.

OUR STORY

In almost a year of being together, we've packed in endless adventures, like traveling across Siargao, Bohol, Siquijor, Olango Island, and even Vietnam. Rooted in deep mutual respect and love, we're ready for our biggest journey yet.

DRESS CODE

What to Wear

Semi-formal, and nothing fancy to buy or rent. Wear what you already have, as long as it fits our colors.

Emerald
Sage
Champagne
Antique gold

For the Ladies

  • A dress, or a nice top with a skirt or pants, in our colors
  • Midi or longer is lovely, but wear what you feel good in
  • Light, breathable fabrics. It will be warm
  • Filipiniana is welcome, never required

For the Gentlemen

  • A polo, long-sleeve polo or polo shirt in our colors
  • Black or dark pants
  • Comfortable closed shoes
  • No need for a barong or a suit

Please Avoid

White, Ivory & cream (Saved for the Bride) Black Tuxedos (Saved for the Groom) Neon Colors Flip-flops

The chapel can be cool inside, so a light shawl or jacket is handy. Our entourage will receive their outfit details separately.
